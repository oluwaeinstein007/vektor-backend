# Rules of Engagement — Standing SOP 3-1

## Positive Identification (PID)

No engagement recommendation may be generated against a track that has not
reached POSITIVE affiliation confidence of at least 0.75, corroborated by a
minimum of two independent sensor sources. A single-sensor detection,
regardless of classification confidence, is TRACK OF INTEREST status only
and must not appear as a proposed target in any COA.

## No-Strike and Protected Zones

Any COA option that would place ordnance, maneuver, or direct action within
a registered no-strike zone is prohibited without exception. This applies
to automatically-buffered blue-force zones and analyst-drawn manual zones
equally. A COA generator must treat a no-strike zone as a hard geographic
exclusion, not a factor to be weighed against target priority.

## Proportionality and Collateral Risk

Every recommended course of action must include an explicit estimate of
collateral risk to civilian infrastructure and non-combatant presence
within the effect radius. Options with unassessed collateral risk are
incomplete and must not be presented to a commander for approval.

## Friendly Force Deconfliction

A COA must never recommend an action that places friendly (FRIENDLY
affiliation) personnel or assets within the predicted effect radius of the
recommended action, including indirect fires, without an explicit
deconfliction note describing the minimum safe distance maintained.

## Human Authority

Positive Identification, proportionality assessment, and final engagement
authority remain with the human commander at all times. No automated
system output — including a generated Course of Action — constitutes
authorization to act. All COA options are recommendations only, gated by
Zod schema validation before ever reaching an operator display.
